# Iris Aerial design context

## North star

Iris Aerial should feel like an engineering and geospatial company that happens to build advanced AI—not an AI template that happens to mention drones. The site must earn trust from infrastructure, government, defence, and enterprise visitors while also making the company attractive to technical talent.

## Visual system

- Survey Navy `#071c2c`: primary text, header, technical surfaces.
- Iris Cyan `#20b6d7`: brand actions and spatial-data cues.
- Flight Blue `#4fc8df`: secondary brand highlight from the supplied mark.
- Signal Orange `#f06a3a`: survey markers, client rail, and rare emphasis only.
- Field Mist `#e9f3f5`: quiet technical backgrounds.
- Paper `#fbfdfd`: primary page surface.
- Display: Space Grotesk; body: Manrope; utility/data: IBM Plex Mono.
- Akshaa owns a distinct product palette inside the wider Iris system: Intelligence Indigo `#101633`, Lens Violet `#6f5cff`, Review Aqua `#45d9d0`, and Alert Coral `#ff7566`. These colors stay scoped to the Akshaa route.
- Corners are measured rather than pill-heavy: 12–24px on panels, 999px only for status chips.
- Global motion is functional: page transitions, reveal-on-scroll, client rail, team-gallery dissolve, and control feedback. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential movement.

## Signature

The hero uses a controlled “project atlas” made from real Iris Aerial case-study imagery: difficult road terrain, a LiDAR terrain surface, and an orthorectified rail corridor. The motion moves between evidence and outputs instead of animating a decorative drone. It includes manual previous, next, and pause controls, and becomes static when reduced motion is preferred.

## Layout language

- Real field photography carries operational credibility; technical overlays are used only when they explain a real review action.
- Homepage proof points and project captions must be traceable to supplied company casework; concise measurements such as corridor length, contour interval, and GSD are used only when present in that source.
- Homepage capability content follows the actual handoff from capture to interpretation to engineering-ready delivery, shown as one connected field board rather than isolated feature cards.
- Solutions is organised by operating environment—rail, road, urban, mining, and water—then ties methods to the engineering outputs each environment needs. Real case imagery and a five-stage survey chain replace the former text-only list.
- Akshaa is the product centrepiece. Its route clearly separates Urban Development from Defence, and both sections explain the need, the response, the review model, and the eventual handoff. Urban uses the supplied infrastructure-segmentation image; Defence uses the supplied aircraft object-detection and temporal-comparison imagery. Evidence images retain their full aspect ratios and sit in spacious panels.
- Team profiles use an editorial people directory with leadership emphasis and named individual portraits from assets/team. Candid culture photography has its own energetic slideshow and photo story so group images are never misattributed to individuals.
- The homepage office map is a compact supporting preview; contact copy and actions retain visual priority.
- Testimonials must remain explicitly marked as sample copy until the client supplies approved quotations and attributions.

## Content principles

- Describe outputs and operating workflows, not vague superlatives.
- Do not publish performance percentages, project counts, certifications, or client claims that are not provided or publicly evidenced.
- “Akshaa” is presented as a human-reviewed geospatial intelligence workflow, not an autonomous black box.
- Unconfirmed team roles remain explicit placeholders rather than invented biography; named portraits are mapped explicitly to their profiles.

## Interaction contract

- Main navigation swaps view sections, updates the URL hash and document title, scrolls to the top, and moves focus to the view heading. There is no standalone Products route; Akshaa owns product and workflow content.
- Every visible button performs an action. No placeholder links use `href="#"`.
- Career applications expand inline for the exact role whose Apply button was selected. The role is displayed as application context rather than exposed as an editable “area of interest” field. The form validates visibly, preserves entered values on errors, and opens a pre-addressed draft to `careers@irisaerial.in`. Because browser mail links cannot attach a local file reliably, the interface explicitly asks applicants to attach their selected resume in their email app.
- Solutions project calls-to-action move visitors to the homepage contact section and prepare the project email link with a relevant subject. Discuss Akshaa opens a draft addressed directly to akshaa@irisaerial.in. This keeps the first interaction reliable in embedded and ordinary browsers.
- The resume picker accepts PDF/DOC/DOCX up to 5 MB and exposes the selected file with a remove action.
- Team-gallery autoplay has pause, previous, next, and dot controls.
- Project-atlas autoplay has pause, previous, next, and dot controls; changing a slide updates its numbered label.

## Page refinements

- The homepage Akshaa preview sits immediately above the office map and displays complete images without cropping.
- Core Technology uses plain-language method explanations and spacious cards with three short stages. Its header uses the supplied drone illustration.
- Careers pairs the header with the supplied rocket illustration and uses a steady grey enquiry email link.
- Solutions offers the supplied case-study PDF as a direct download; the delivery-chain heading uses the full available width.

- Solutions Project fit section uses the requested magenta `#ac2170`, scoped to `.solutions-project-fit`, with white text.
- The homepage Akshaa primary preview and Akshaa hero use the supplied `assets/akshaa/collage.png` object-detection collage, displayed in full without cropping.
