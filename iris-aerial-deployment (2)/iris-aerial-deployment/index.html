How the work moves

A controlled survey chain, not a collection of sensors.

The supplied casework follows a repeatable logic: understand the design question, establish control, acquire complete coverage, validate the model, and deliver information in the engineer’s working format.

Control / 01

Fix the survey to the ground

Reconnaissance, datum, benchmarks, GCPs, checkpoints, and corridor limits are planned before the primary acquisition begins.

DGPS/RTK · Total station · Levelling · GNSS/IMU
Capture / 02

Match the sensor to the terrain

RGB photogrammetry, LiDAR, satellite stereo, or ground survey is selected around visibility, access, coverage, and required detail.

Flight paths · ROW coverage · Overlap · Field QA
Deliver / 03

Build the engineer’s working base

Point clouds are calibrated and classified, imagery is orthorectified, terrain models are checked, and outputs are prepared for CAD and GIS review.

DTM/DSM · Contours · L-sections · Cross-sections · Base maps

Akshaa / Geospatial visual intelligence

One intelligence layer. Two critical environments.

Akshaa connects imagery, location, time, task-specific detection, and human review. Urban teams use it to understand growth and assets; authorised defence teams use it to find, compare, and assess strategic observations.

ObserveLocateCompareReview
Akshaa for Urban Development

Give every site, asset, and change a place on the map.

Urban evidence often lives across survey imagery, spreadsheets, GIS files, inspection notes, and repeated site visits. Akshaa brings those observations into one reviewable spatial workflow.

Why it is needed

Growth moves faster than fragmented records.

Manual comparison makes it difficult to track new construction, missing assets, encroachment, corridor conditions, or progress across large areas and repeated captures.

  • Large image volumes are slow to inspect consistently
  • Changes lose meaning when location and date are separated
  • Planning teams need evidence they can trace back to source
How Akshaa responds
Ingest

Bring in georeferenced drone, aerial, satellite, and project imagery with time and area context.

Surface

Use task-specific models and spatial rules to locate objects, conditions, and change candidates.

Confirm

Keep the source visible while reviewers accept, reject, annotate, and export observations.

Asset intelligence

Locate and organise roads, buildings, utilities, vegetation, and project-specific features.

Temporal change

Compare aligned captures and keep new, missing, or altered observations tied to time.

Progress evidence

Review construction and site change against the previous capture or planned context.

GIS-ready handoff

Move confirmed observations into maps, inventories, reports, and operating workflows.

Akshaa for Defence

Reduce the search area. Preserve analyst judgement.

Large optical scenes and repeated captures create more visual information than an analyst should have to scan unaided. Akshaa structures that workload without hiding the imagery or the reasoning trail.

Why it is needed

Attention should go to the cue that deserves review.

Manual scanning across large imagery sets can make subtle positional, structural, and asset changes difficult to locate and prioritise consistently.

Akshaa keeps four things togetherSource imageTime and locationModel cueReviewer decision
Detect

Find and subclassify assets

Task-specific models locate relevant objects across optical scenes and refine broad detections into useful asset classes.

Scope · aircraft / ships / vehicles / infrastructure
Compare

Read positional and structural change

Time-separated captures are aligned so newly observed, absent, static, and changed objects can be reviewed together.

Human-in-the-loop by design

Akshaa accelerates visual review and structures evidence; it does not replace authorised judgement. Findings remain connected to source imagery and should be corroborated before operational use.

Core technology / How we work

Choose the method for the decision.

We choose the right tools for your site and the information you need. Drones capture detail, satellites cover large areas, and ground instruments check positions. We turn those measurements into maps, models, and drawings your team can use.

01 / 3D capture

LiDAR

LiDAR uses laser pulses to measure the shape and height of the ground. It is useful for road corridors, uneven terrain, and sites with vegetation.

PlanChoose the survey area, flight route, and level of detail needed.
ProcessCombine the laser measurements and separate ground points from buildings and vegetation.
DeliverCreate 3D point clouds, ground models, contours, and engineering sections.
02 / Image geometry

Photogrammetry

Photogrammetry turns overlapping photographs into measurable maps and 3D models. It helps teams inspect sites and compare construction progress.

CaptureTake overlapping images and measure reference points on the ground.
BuildMatch features across photographs to build a consistent map and 3D surface.
DeliverProvide a corrected aerial map, surface model, and progress views.
03 / Regional context

Satellite sensing

Satellite images help us understand large areas without visiting every location. They support route planning, land-use mapping, and change monitoring.

SelectChoose images with the right date, detail, and cloud coverage.
CompareAlign the images and identify land features or changes over time.
DeliverProvide planning maps and GIS layers with the source imagery documented.
04 / Survey control

DGPS & ETS

DGPS uses satellite positioning, while an electronic total station measures angles and distances. Together, they give aerial maps reliable ground reference points.

Set upEstablish measured reference points and survey boundaries.
MeasureRecord positions, levels, and features across the site.
CheckCheck the measurements and prepare coordinates, plans, and sections.
05 / Subsurface

Ground-penetrating radar

Ground-penetrating radar sends signals below the surface to help locate buried utilities, layers, and possible voids without digging.

PlanChoose the survey lines and equipment for the expected depth.
ReadReview the reflected signals alongside site records and drawings.
ReportMap possible underground features and explain where further checks are needed.
06 / Water systems

Hydrography

Hydrography measures the shape and depth of underwater ground. It supports work on rivers, reservoirs, channels, and dams.

PrepareSet survey routes and check water levels and equipment.
SurveyMeasure water depth and position along the planned routes.
DeliverCreate depth maps, underwater contours, and capacity estimates.
07 / Decision layer

GIS, AI & Akshaa

GIS organises information on a map. AI and Akshaa help find features and changes in imagery, while people review the results before they are used.

OrganiseConnect imagery, measurements, and records to their locations.
ReviewFind possible objects or changes and let reviewers confirm the evidence.
ShareDeliver map layers, asset lists, reports, or dashboards your team can use.
